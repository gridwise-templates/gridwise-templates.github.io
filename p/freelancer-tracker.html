# Frequently Asked Questions — Freelancer Income, Expense & Invoice Tracker

### 1. Is this compatible with Excel and Google Sheets?
Yes, this template is fully tested and functional in both Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.

### 2. How do I generate an invoice from the tracker?
Navigate to the **Invoice** tab, select your client from the dropdown menu, and enter the project milestone details. The invoice pulls itemized figures automatically and is ready to export as a PDF via **File > Print > Save as PDF**.

### 3. How do I track expenses for tax purposes?
Log each business expense on the **Expenses** tab, selecting the appropriate tax category (e.g., Software, Equipment, Travel). The summary dashboard aggregates these totals automatically for quarterly and annual reporting.

### 4. What currency settings are supported?
The default currency is Euros (€), but you can easily change the number formatting to USD ($), GBP (£), or any other currency via your spreadsheet software's number formatting menu.

### 5. My formulas are not calculating automatically. What is wrong?
Check that automatic calculation is enabled in your software. In Excel, go to **Formulas > Calculation Options** and set it to **Automatic**. In Google Sheets, calculation settings are found under **File > Settings > Calculation**.
