# Frequently Asked Questions — Personal Budget & Savings Planner

### 1. Does this template work in both Excel and Google Sheets?
Yes! You can use this template in Microsoft Excel (desktop or web) as well as Google Sheets. For Google Sheets, simply upload the `.xlsx` file into your Google Drive and open it. All formulas and formatting are fully compatible with both platforms.

### 2. How do I change the currency symbol from Euros (€) to Dollars ($) or Pounds (£)?
Highlight the cells containing financial figures, right-click and select **Format Cells** (in Excel) or go to **Format > Number > Custom currency** (in Google Sheets), and select your preferred currency symbol.

### 3. Can I customize the budget categories?
Yes, all category names and dropdown lists are fully editable. You can rename, add, or delete categories to fit your personal financial situation. Do not delete entire rows or columns with active formula blocks unless you are familiar with Excel formulas; instead, simply clear the text or overwrite category titles.

### 4. What should I do if a formula shows an error (like `#NAME?` or `#REF!`)?
This usually happens if the file is opened in an older, unsupported version of spreadsheet software. Ensure you are using a modern version of Excel (2016 or newer) or Google Sheets. If the error persists, re-download the clean original file from your purchase link.

### 5. How do I print or export my monthly report to PDF?
Select the specific sheet or table you wish to print, go to **File > Print**, and choose **Fit to Page** or **Fit to Width** under the scaling options to ensure a clean, professional PDF export.
