# Frequently Asked Questions — Debt Payoff Calculator

### 1. Does this calculator work in Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel?
Yes, the Debt Payoff Calculator is fully optimized for both Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets. Simply upload the `.xlsx` file to Google Drive or open it directly in Excel.

### 2. How do I add more debts to the tracker?
The built-in table is pre-formatted for up to 10 distinct debts. If you have fewer debts, simply leave the remaining rows blank; the totals and payoff schedules will automatically adjust. 

### 3. Can I use extra monthly payments in the calculations?
Yes! The dashboard includes dedicated input fields for extra monthly contributions. The schedule dynamically recalculates your debt-free date and total interest saved in real time.

### 4. Why are my monthly balances showing `#REF!` errors?
Ensure you have not deleted entire rows in the master debt table. The monthly schedule rows link directly to the summary cells above. If a reference breaks, re-download the original file.

### 5. Can I print my payoff schedule for my physical planner?
Yes. Go to **File > Print**, select **Fit to page width**, and choose portrait or landscape depending on your preference.
