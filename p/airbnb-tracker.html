## 3. Handling Currency Conversions & Formatting

Short-term rental hosts operating across borders or listing on multiple global platforms often need custom currency formatting.

* **Changing Currency Symbols (Excel & Google Sheets):**
  1. Highlight the cells containing financial figures (Income, Expenses, Net Profit).
  2. In Excel: Go to the **Home** tab, locate the **Number** group, and click the Currency dropdown ($ / € / £ / ¥) or select *Format Cells > Currency*.
  3. In Google Sheets: Go to the top menu and click **Format > Number > Currency** (or choose *Custom currency* for specialized symbols).
* **Handling Multiple Currencies:** If you receive payouts in different currencies (e.g., USD for US listings and EUR for European listings), we recommend establishing a standardized base currency in the Setup tab and converting incoming payouts using your platform payout exchange rate before logging them into the tracker.

## 5. Common Error Resolution & Troubleshooting

### Q: I see `#REF!` or `#VALUE!` errors in summary cells. What happened?
* **Cause:** This typically occurs if a formula reference was accidentally deleted or if a row was deleted while formulas were referencing it.
* **Solution:** Press `Ctrl + Z` (or `Cmd + Z` on Mac) to undo the deletion. If the error persists, re-download a fresh copy of the master template from your purchase link.

### Q: The dropdown menus for properties or expense categories are not working.
* **Cause:** Data validation rules may have been altered during editing.
* **Solution:** Select the affected cells, go to **Data > Data Validation**, and ensure the source range points correctly to your property inventory list in the Setup tab.

### Q: Why isn't my net profit updating when I enter an expense?
* **Cause:** Ensure that both the amount and a valid expense category/property have been selected. If automatic calculation is disabled in your Excel application, press `F9` to recalculate formulas.
